Support: Proportion of transactions containing these products together (e.g., 0.2216 = 22.16% of transactions). Confidence: Probability that someone buying Product 1 also buys Product 2 (e.g., 0.9900 = 99% likelihood). Lift: How much more likely Product 2 is purchased with Product 1 (higher = stronger association).
